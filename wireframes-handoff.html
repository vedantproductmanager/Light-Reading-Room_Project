If the reader wants to ask aloud

Ask is an optional microphone control beside playback. The reader starts each listening turn.

  1. Tap AskBook audio pauses immediately. The pointer stays put and the prompt reads “Listening…” with a Cancel action.
  2. “What does boughs mean in Hindi?”Show the recognized word and open the same definition sheet from 09. If two words sound alike, show the matches so the reader can choose.
  3. “Resume reading”Tap Ask again to say this, or tap Resume reading directly. The microphone and any definition audio stop before the book resumes at 10.

If the microphone is unavailable or the word isn’t recognized, keep the page paused and offer “Tap the word on the page.” If a definition is unavailable, leave Resume reading visible.

How much text fits while listening?

Compare two layouts at the same phone width and the same reading size. This original prose sample is separate from the short poem used in the walkthrough.

Proposed fit

Target roughly 85–100 visible words on a typical phone when the text stays readable and controls fit. Let the count drop toward 50 on smaller screens or at larger text sizes. In a fixed-layout PDF, let readers zoom the page and follow the visible part. A synced text view can reflow where the edition supports it.

Page and pointer rules

  • Show one PDF page at a timeThe paper area shows the original page layout. The app chrome stays outside it. Let a reader zoom or pan a dense page without moving their saved reading position.
  • Sync to the text on the pageEach narrated passage maps to a page and a line on its selectable text layer. Use a checked text layer for scanned pages before offering a synchronized pointer.
  • Handle a manual turnSwipe or use the page arrows. Pause playback and set the start of the destination page as the next listening point.
  • Keep exits predictableBack saves the page and spoken location. Contents opens a chapter list and returns to the reader at the chosen page. At the end of the book, offer a return to Home.

Acceptance notes

Expected behavior for implementation and QA.

Pause, turn, and resume

Freeze the cue on pause. Resume replays the interrupted line. Dismiss a sheet with narration paused. An automatic turn keeps playing. A manual turn pauses at the destination page.

Buffering and ending

Keep the visible page while narration loads. If it stalls, offer Retry and silent reading. Persist the last confirmed line when backgrounded. At the end of playback, show Book complete and Back to Home. A silent reader can choose Mark as finished.

Define a word

Pause the book before definition audio or voice capture begins. For ambiguous speech, show likely matches. If a term or language is unavailable, leave Resume visible and offer the original context.

Selection and saved anchors

Support word, line, and paragraph selection. Save the edition, page, and text anchor. If an edition changes, show the saved excerpt. Open a suggested page only after the reader confirms. Keep the draft if saving fails.

Profile and comparison

Use completed and marked-as-read titles for level. Use in-app pages over the same window for pace and rank. Show the date range and cohort. With insufficient benchmark data, show Unavailable. Ties share a rank.

History and paths

A marked read updates history once and offers Undo. A started path returns to its current title. A completed path offers another topic. For an unavailable title, keep the path position and offer a replacement.

Viewport and accessibility

Use at least 44 px touch targets in the implemented app. Allow zoom and larger text. Keep actions reachable when the keyboard opens. Restore focus on sheet close. Pair color with labels or shapes.

Offline and missing content

Open cached pages offline where available. Preserve unsent edits for retry. If a page fails to load, offer Retry and a route back to filtered Library or Saved. Empty Saved explains how to add the first item.

Decisions for implementation

Opening a book
Open silently. Restore the saved edition, page, and line when returning. If there is no saved point, start at the first readable page. Back returns to the source list or Home with its previous position.
Changing the active page
When the reader turns a page, leaves the reader, switches books, or cancels loading, discard late responses from the previous playback request. Only the current book and page may restart narration.
Choosing between audio sources
Only one source plays at a time. Starting a definition or voice capture stops the book. Resume stops the definition and microphone first. If the system interrupts audio, switch to Paused and let the reader resume.
Recording history and progress
Treat confirmed actions as idempotent. A book or volume counted as finished stays one entry across retries and repeat taps. Keep externally marked reading out of in-app page totals. Count each edition and page once per local day after its final audio cue or a forward turn in silent reading.
Keeping the reader oriented
When a saved anchor maps to an exact match, open it highlighted and paused. When the text anchor can’t be matched, show the saved excerpt, edition details, and a way back. A failed note write retains the draft.
Reporting comparisons
Keep timeframe, cohort, and units visible. For a reader with no activity, show the empty state. With a valid window but no comparable peers, keep personal pace and leave rank unavailable.
Confirm before a live release

Book and narration rights and availability, selectable-text and audio alignment quality, language and voice support, and the source and minimum cohort size for national and peer comparisons. Keep the demo values labeled until real inputs are approved.
